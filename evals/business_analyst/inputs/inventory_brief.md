# Stock control - project brief (synthetic)

**Customer:** Example Distribution FZE, a building-materials distributor with about 80 staff.

## Background
Stock is held in one central warehouse in Jebel Ali. Stock levels are kept in spreadsheets updated at the end of
each day, so sales staff often promise goods that are not on the shelf.

## What the customer wants
- Warehouse staff record goods received and goods dispatched as they happen.
- Sales staff can see current stock for each product before confirming an order.
- Reorder alerts when stock is low.
- A monthly stock valuation report for finance.

## Constraints and context
- The company uses an accounting package for invoicing; how it would connect is not known yet.
- Warehouse staff use handheld devices; the warehouse has patchy Wi-Fi in some aisles.
- The budget is fixed and the first version should be live in four months.
