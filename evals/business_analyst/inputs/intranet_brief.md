# Staff intranet - project brief (synthetic)

**Customer:** Example Holdings LLC, a group of five companies with about 600 staff in Dubai and Riyadh.

## Background
Each company has its own shared drives and email newsletters. Staff cannot find policies, and the group cannot
reach everyone with one announcement.

## What the customer wants
- One intranet for the whole group with news, policies and a staff directory.
- Each company can publish its own news, and group HR can publish to everyone.
- Staff can search for policies and people.

## Constraints and context
- The group uses Microsoft 365.
- Arabic and English are both used.
- Existing policies are spread across five shared drives in different formats.
